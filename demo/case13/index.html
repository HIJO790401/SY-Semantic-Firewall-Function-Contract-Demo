01 / 主體的兩個追問

不是加權或不加權，先交出必要不變量。

  1. 大農場到什麼門檻可用產權算3-4%？
  2. 小農場的產權3-4%標準是什麼？

必要不變量沒有定義，問題就沒有取得有效判決的資格。偏好、說服與繼續生成都不能補前提。在這個條件下繼續運算，是算力浪費與結構風險。

01 / THE PRINCIPAL’S TWO QUESTIONS

Before weighting or non-weighting, provide the necessary invariants.

  1. At what threshold may a large farm use property rights to calculate 3–4%?
  2. What is the standard for the small farm’s 3–4% property rights?

Undefined necessary invariants do not qualify for a valid judgment. Preference, persuasion and further generation cannot fill missing premises. Continuing computation under that condition is wasted compute and structural risk.

我的既有主權判決效力條款

判決的有效性不依賴模型。模型可用於輸入轉換與表述，其輸出受契約約束，不得改變判詞，亦不得取得判決效力。

判詞、必要不變量、失效條件及返回責任由契約固定；模型不得補造、改判或自行授權。未取得合法函式回傳，就不得產生有效交付或繼續執行。

My existing Sovereign Verdict Validity Clause

The validity of a judgment does not depend on the model. A model may be used for input transformation and expression; its output is bound by contract, may not alter the verdict, and may not acquire the effect of a judgment.

The contract fixes the verdict, necessary invariants, invalidation conditions, and return responsibility. The model may not fabricate premises, alter the verdict, or authorize itself. Without a valid function return, there can be no valid delivery or continued execution.
